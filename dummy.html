Why Australian PR?

Before how, it’s why to apply for Australian PR. With the world-class healthcare, education, and economic opportunities, the Australian PR comes with various advantages of security and stability. PR holders are free to live, work and study in country. They can also sponsor their relatives and eventually apply for the citizenship.

Pathways To Transition From Temporary To Permanent:

Skilled Migration Pathways:

These visas allow workers to attain experience in Australia and get them eligible for the permanent residency pathways like Skilled Independent Visa (subclass 189), Skilled Nominated Visa (subclass 190), and Skilled Regional Visa (subclass 191) that have different requirements.

Employer-Sponsored Pathway:

‍Employers can send their highly capable and eligible employees for PR through Employer Nomination Scheme (subclass 186) that gives direct entry for TSS visa holders and Regional Sponsored Migration Scheme (subclass 187) which is for regional workers.

Business and Investment Pathway:

Individuals with outstanding business plan, specific requirements and international achievements can apply for Business Innovation and Investment Program that eventually give them permanent residency after certain period of time (generally after 5 years).

‍Family Sponsored Pathway:‍

Individuals living with their family on a temporary visa can apply for permanent residency with the help of Partner Visa (subclass 820/801) which is for spouses of Australian partners of PR holders and Parent Visa (subclass 108/804) which is for parents of Australia citizens or PR holders.
